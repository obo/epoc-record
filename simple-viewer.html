simple-viewer

Loads two epoc-record/epoc-reread-format CSVs entirely client-side (no server, no upload) and lets you browse, zoom, pan and shift one against the other, with a live Pearson correlation over the visible window. Parsing and rendering use typed arrays and per-pixel decimation so this scales from a 3-minute test capture up to a full lecture-length recording (~1-2h).
